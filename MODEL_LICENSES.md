## Built-in multilingual translation

Every desktop installer includes `facebook/m2m100_418M` converted to CTranslate2 INT8 (`m2m100-418M-int8`), revision `55c2e61bbf05dfb8d7abccdc3fae6fc8512fd636`. Its model card declares the MIT license. The original model card, MIT license notice, pinned source hash and per-file SHA-256 manifest ship inside `M2M100-418M-int8.zip`. This translation component is installed automatically and is independent of optional Sony audio model selections.

Source: https://huggingface.co/facebook/m2m100_418M
